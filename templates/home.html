ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Food Supply Chains More Transparent

FoodSafe is an academic project that combines Python full-stack development, QR-code verification, machine learning, and hash-chain integrity checking.

The system aims to make registered product information and supply-chain records easier to access and verify.

Important: A valid hash chain confirms consistency between recorded data and its hashes. It does not independently prove food authenticity, food safety, or the absence of adulteration. QR codes provide access to information but do not guarantee its truthfulness.
